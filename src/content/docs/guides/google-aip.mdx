Google's **API Improvement Proposals** ([google.aip.dev](https://google.aip.dev/)) are a numbered set of design documents behind thousands of Google APIs. They're built around gRPC and protocol buffers, but map onto HTTP/JSON, and they make some different choices from Zalando's guide. That makes them a perfect second guide.

</Hook>

## What to expect

- `<Rule guide="google">` boxes added to the existing lessons, right next to Zalando's.
- A comparison of the places where the two guides disagree, and why.
- A few topics Google covers in depth: resource names, standard vs. custom methods, field masks, long-running operations.

A sneak peek is already on [How guides compare](/guides/).
